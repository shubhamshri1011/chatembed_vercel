Frequently Asked Questions

How does the 20% recurring monthly partner commission work?

When you refer a client or onboard them via your partner referral link, you receive 20% of their subscription fee every single month for the lifetime of their account. Payouts are settled automatically to your Indian bank account via NEFT/UPI on the 10th of every month.

Can we white-label the widget with our agency branding?

Yes. On agency partner tiers, you can remove 'Powered by EmbedEase' completely and replace it with 'Powered by [Your Agency Name]' with custom backlink routing, or remove branding entirely.

Who is eligible for the EmbedEase Agency Partner Program?

Digital marketing agencies, freelance performance marketers, Shopify developers, WordPress web designers, and business consultants operating in India or managing Indian client portfolios are eligible.
