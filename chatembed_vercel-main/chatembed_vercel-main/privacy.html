Privacy Policy

How Roopya Technologies collects, secures, and respects personal data when you use the EmbedEase platform and embed widgets on your web properties.

1. Overview & Data Fiduciary Details

This Privacy Policy applies to the software platform EmbedEase, operated and owned by Roopya Technologies (“Company”, “we”, “our”, or “us”), registered under the laws of India.

For inquiries regarding data collection, processing, or data principal rights under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act 2023), you may reach our Data Protection Officer at support@embedease.in.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in two capacities: as a Data Fiduciary for account holders, and as a Data Processor on behalf of our business clients who deploy widgets on their websites.

  • Account Credentials: Full name, verified business email, organization name, and billing details provided during registration.
  • Website End-User Lead Information: When an end user submits contact details via the chat widget (name, telephone number, email, or inquiry message), this data is encrypted and passed directly to your EmbedEase account dashboard or webhook integrations.
  • Attribution & Technical Analytics: Referrer URLs, UTM tags (source, medium, campaign), device class, browser type, and geographic country/city level data. We do not log raw keystrokes or sensitive biometric tokens.
  • Payment Processing: Transactions are handled securely via PCI-DSS Level 1 certified gateways (e.g. Razorpay). EmbedEase never stores full credit or debit card numbers on our servers.

3. Purpose and Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal data only when lawful grounds exist:

  • To provide, calibrate, and deliver embeddable chat and voice widget infrastructure.
  • To generate conversation summaries and deliver webhook payloads to your CRM.
  • To detect abuse, bot spoofing, and fraud via rate limiting and automated threat filters.
  • To comply with statutory tax, audit, and legal obligations in India.

4. Data Security & Storage Architecture

All database communications are encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and at rest with AES-256. Row Level Security (RLS) policies isolate each client’s chat logs and lead data. Data processing servers are hosted in SOC 2 Type II certified cloud facilities.

5. Your Rights as a Data Principal

Under the DPDP Act 2023 and applicable privacy standards, you are entitled to:

  • Request a summary of personal data being processed.
  • Request correction or erasure of incomplete or inaccurate data.
  • Withdraw consent for optional analytics or marketing communications.
  • Nominate another individual to exercise rights in event of death or incapacity.
