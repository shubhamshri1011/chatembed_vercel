App install campaigns ka budget kaise optimize karein: Checklist

To avoid burning through marketing capital in the first 72 hours of campaign launch, adhere strictly to this configuration checklist:

1

Upload All 5 Video Asset Orientations

Never launch an App Campaign with only landscape (16:9) video. YouTube Shorts and mobile display require vertical portrait (9:16) and square (1:1) video creatives. Google penalizes accounts with incomplete asset diversity with higher bidding CPMs.

2

Set Daily Budget to At Least 50x Target CPI

If your target CPI is ₹30, setting a daily budget of ₹500 restricts the algorithm from exploring traffic clusters. Google’s bidding model needs a minimum budget of 50x your target CPI (₹1,500/day per campaign) to exit the learning phase in under 5 days.

3

Combine App Campaigns with Website Attributions

Many users discover your app through your website first. Using EmbedEase AI Chatbot, you can send an automated SMS or WhatsApp download link directly to visitor phones, converting website traffic into verified app installs.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Firebase tracking link with Google Ads App Install campaigns?

By linking your Google Play Console and Firebase project to Google Ads, the app_open, first_open, and custom in-app conversion events (such as complete_registration or in_app_purchase) automatically sync into Google Ads conversion tracking without needing third-party SDKs.

What is the average Cost Per Install (CPI) in India across industries?

In India, average CPI ranges between ₹15 to ₹45 for casual gaming and content apps, ₹25 to ₹60 for D2C e-commerce, ₹80 to ₹180 for Fintech and loan applications, and ₹90 to ₹220 for B2B and EdTech platforms. Actual CPI depends heavily on creative asset quality and bid strategy.

Should I bid for Target CPI (tCPI) or In-App Actions (tCPA)?

For brand new apps with fewer than 50 conversions, start with Target CPI (App Installs volume) to build machine learning conversion data. Once your app records 30 to 50 in-app actions per day, switch to Target CPA focused on registration or purchase to eliminate low-intent churn.

App install campaigns ka budget kaise optimize karein to reduce uninstalls?

Focus heavily on portrait video creatives (9:16) demonstrating the real inside UI of your app. Misleading creatives cause high day-1 uninstalls. Additionally, configure deep links (App Links / Universal Links) so users landing from ads immediately enter the relevant onboarding screen rather than a generic home screen.
