Frequently Asked Questions

Who owns the ad accounts and customer data?
You own 100% of your ad accounts, WhatsApp API numbers, and customer data. We never hold client accounts hostage. If you ever leave EmbedEase, all your assets and history remain entirely yours.
How does the chatbot prevent hallucinations?
Our chatbot utilizes strict Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). It only answers questions using your approved business knowledge base. If information is missing, it politely informs the visitor and requests their contact details for a human callback.
Can I use my existing WhatsApp Business number?
Yes. You can connect your official Meta WhatsApp Cloud API number. We guide you through the Meta Business verification process and template approvals step-by-step.
How fast can we go live?
The AI chatbot can be installed and trained on your website in under 10 minutes. Custom ad campaigns and WhatsApp Cloud API verification typically go live within 24–48 hours.
How does attribution tracking work without third-party cookies?
EmbedEase uses first-party persistence via our lightweight widget script. It stores UTM parameters, GCLID (Google Click ID), and FBCLID (Meta Click ID) directly in the visitor session and ties them permanently to submitted leads.
