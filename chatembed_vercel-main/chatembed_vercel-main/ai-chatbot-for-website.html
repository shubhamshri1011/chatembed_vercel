Frequently Asked Questions

How does EmbedEase prevent AI chatbot hallucinations on my website?

EmbedEase uses strict Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). The AI assistant is constrained to retrieve verified information exclusively from your uploaded documents, product catalogs, and URLs. If an answer cannot be found in your approved business knowledge base, the bot politely states it doesn't have that detail and captures the visitor's WhatsApp number or email for a human callback.

Why is a zero-iframe chatbot faster than Voiceflow or traditional widgets?

Traditional chatbot builders load an entire browser inside an iframe, creating a separate rendering context, pulling 1.5MB to 3MB of duplicated runtime libraries, and destroying Core Web Vitals (LCP and INP). EmbedEase is engineered in pure, vanilla JavaScript weighing under 12KB gzip with direct Shadow DOM isolation. It consumes zero extra render threads and does not affect your Google Lighthouse score.

Can the chatbot hand off conversations directly to my official WhatsApp number?

Yes. Visitors can click a one-tap WhatsApp button inside the chat window. EmbedEase preserves the entire conversation history, visitor intent score, and advertising attribution parameters (UTMs, GCLID, FBCLID) so your sales team has full context upon receiving the WhatsApp chat.
