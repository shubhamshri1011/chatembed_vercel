7-Day Money-Back Guarantee

Refund & Cancellation

We want you to build with complete confidence. Here is how our transparent refund and cancellation process works.

1. 7-Day Money-Back Guarantee

If you subscribe to an EmbedEase paid plan (Starter, Pro, or Agency) and determine within the first 7 calendar days of initial payment that the software does not suit your integration requirements, we will issue a full 100% refund, no questions asked.

2. Subscription Cancellation

You may cancel your recurring subscription at any time directly through your EmbedEase console dashboard or by contacting support:

  • Upon cancellation, your subscription remains active until the end of the current paid billing cycle.
  • You will not be billed on future billing dates.
  • Your bots and leads will transition to the Free Tier upon expiration without data deletion.

3. How to Request a Refund

To initiate a refund under the 7-day guarantee:

  1. Send an email to support@embedease.in from your registered account email.
  2. Include your Razorpay Payment ID or Invoice Number.
  3. Our finance desk will confirm the request within 24 business hours.
  4. Funds will be returned to the original payment source (UPI, Credit/Debit Card, Netbanking) within 5 to 7 business days per banking settlement norms.

4. Questions & Support Assistance

Need help configuring your widget before making a cancellation decision? Reach our engineering team directly via our Contact page or email support@embedease.in.
