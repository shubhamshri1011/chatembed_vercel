Frequently Asked Questions

Why do Google Analytics 4 (GA4) and Meta Ads show conflicting lead numbers?

GA4 relies on client-side session cookies that are frequently erased by browser privacy features (like Apple Safari ITP and iOS 14.5+ prompt blocks), losing 30% to 40% of conversion source data. Meta claims credit using broad view-through windows. EmbedEase uses first-party persistent session storage and server-side capture, recording exact GCLID, FBCLID, and UTM tags directly onto the lead's database record.

How does EmbedEase calculate True Cost Per Lead (CPL)?

True CPL is calculated by dividing verified advertising spend for an individual campaign or ad set by the exact count of qualified leads verified by your CRM or sales reps. Unlike agency blended averages that mix cheap bot clicks with real inquiries, EmbedEase gives you granular CPL per keyword, creative, and audience.

Yes. Every lead captured via our widget, website forms, or WhatsApp can be instantly synced via native webhooks, Zapier, Make, or n8n into HubSpot, Zoho CRM, Salesforce, LeadSquared, or Google Sheets with all UTM parameters intact.
