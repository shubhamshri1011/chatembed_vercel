Step 1

Quickstart: The 1-Line Embed

Adding EmbedEase to your website requires pasting one asynchronous script tag directly before the closing </body> tag of your HTML. 

<script src="https://embedease.in/widget.js"
  data-bot-id="YOUR_BOT_ID"
  data-color="#0E6B4D"
  data-name="Aria"
  data-whatsapp="919425625642"
  data-welcome="Hi! How can I help your business today?">
</script>
1

Copy Your Bot ID

Navigate to your Console Dashboard, select your bot, and copy its unique UUID or embed snippet.

2

Paste into Your Site Footer

Paste the code into your site template immediately before </body>. Because it is non-blocking, it will not impact your Core Web Vitals or page load speed.

3

Verify Real-Time Appearance

Refresh your site in an incognito window. The floating bubble will appear in the bottom-right corner ready for interaction.

Step 2

Platform Installation Guides

Choose your hosting environment or CMS platform for tailored instructions:

WordPress

  1. Log in to WordPress Admin.
  2. Install the free "Header and Footer Scripts" plugin (or go to Appearance → Theme File Editor).
  3. Paste the EmbedEase script into the Footer Scripts box.
  4. Click Save Changes.

Shopify

  1. Go to Online Store → Themes in Shopify Admin.
  2. Click the three dots (···) → Edit Code.
  3. Select theme.liquid under Layout.
  4. Scroll to the bottom and paste before </body>.
  5. Click Save.

Webflow

  1. Open Webflow Designer → Project Settings.
  2. Click the Custom Code tab.
  3. Paste the snippet in Footer Code.
  4. Publish to your production domain.

Framer

  1. Open Project Settings → General.
  2. Scroll to Custom Code.
  3. Paste the snippet in the End of <body> tag box.
  4. Click Save and Publish.

Wix

  1. Go to Settings → Custom Code in your Wix dashboard.
  2. Click + Add Custom Code.
  3. Paste your script and set placement to Body - end.
  4. Apply to All Pages.

React / Next.js

  1. In your root layout (app/layout.tsx or pages/_document.tsx):
  2. Add <Script src="https://embedease.in/widget.js" ... /> with strategy="lazyOnload".
  3. Verify in browser console.
Step 3

AI Agents & Voice Integrations

EmbedEase connects with any conversation backend through simple webhook relaying or platform proxying:

Voiceflow

Voiceflow DM API

In your bot settings on EmbedEase, select "Voiceflow" as engine and enter your VF_PROJECT_API_KEY. Dialog state is synchronized seamlessly.

Botpress

Botpress Cloud Webhook

Provide your Botpress Webhook URL and Bot ID in settings. End-user inquiries are routed directly to your Botpress flow cards.

Flowise AI

Flowise Prediction Endpoint

Connect your self-hosted or cloud Flowise chatflow by entering the Prediction URL. Supports LangChain agents and custom vector stores.

Voice AI

Bland.ai & Retell AI

Enable real-time voice call initiation directly from the chat widget. Visitors can click "Request Instant Voice Call" with zero latency.

Step 4

Lead Capture & WhatsApp Direct Desk

When active, the widget gathers prospective buyer contact details and supports immediate WhatsApp conversation handoff:

  • Automated In-Chat Lead Form: After 3 conversational turns or upon user interest, an un-intrusive prompt asks for work email or phone number.
  • One-Click WhatsApp Handoff: By adding data-whatsapp="91XXXXXXXXXX", a prominent WhatsApp button lets buyers transition to WhatsApp with full chat context pre-filled.
  • Leads CRM Kanban: View incoming contacts grouped by Hot, Warm, and Cold on your dashboard, or export to CSV.
FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is the widget not rendering on my site?

Verify that: (1) The script tag is placed before </body> and not inside <head>; (2) The data-bot-id matches your bot's ID in the dashboard; (3) Your bot status is set to "Live".

Does the widget slow down my website?

No. EmbedEase is lightweight (< 28KB compressed), loads asynchronously from edge CDN nodes, and executes without blocking DOM rendering or triggering layout shifts (CLS).

Can I customize the primary color to match my exact brand?

Yes. Use the Widget Builder or set data-color="#YOURHEX" on the script tag.
